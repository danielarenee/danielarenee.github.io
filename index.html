01 Preprint

Mathematical epidemiology · KAUST

Compartmental Epidemiological Models with Infection-Driven Immune Escape

Daniela R. Ramírez-Gutiérrez, Lajos Lóczi, Carlos Muñoz-Moncayo & David I. Ketcheson · 2026 · Submitted to the Journal of Mathematical Biology

Developed a compartmental SIRCm model in which infection-linked feedback affects waning immunity and immune escape. The coauthored study compares prevalence-driven and transmission-driven variants and analyzes when endemic equilibria become unstable, even without seasonal forcing.

  • Compartmental models
  • Dynamical systems
  • Immune escape

02 In progress

Environmental data · UDLAP

Wastewater treatment design for the Río Atoyac basin

Interdisciplinary research team · Aug 2026–present

The Río Atoyac, which runs through Tlaxcala and Puebla, is considered one of the most polluted rivers in Mexico, receiving industrial and municipal wastewater along its course. I’m analyzing laboratory water-quality measurements to estimate textile-industry contamination and support the design of a treatment train for a river-remediation initiative.

  • Environmental data
  • Treatment design

03 Honors thesis · ongoing

Operations research · UDLAP

Optimal control of environmental pollution via partially observable Markov decision processes

Supervised by Prof. Rubén Blancas Rivera · Feb 2026–present

Developing a partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP) to control pollution in environmental systems where the true pollution level can’t be measured directly. Dynamic programming is used to simulate policies that decide how to act based on noisy measurements.

  • POMDP
  • Dynamic programming
  • Optimal control
04 Presented · Oct 2026

Network science

An Algorithm for Constructing Inter-City Road Connectivity Networks

Daniela R. Ramírez-Gutiérrez & Héctor Maravillo · 2026

Designed an algorithm to build inter-city connectivity networks from detailed road graphs. The approach combines network contraction with multi-source Dijkstra and is applied to Mexico’s national road network. Presented at the XIV Congress of the Mexican Society for Operations Research (Universidad de Monterrey, 7–9 October 2026).

  • Graph algorithms
  • Network contraction
  • Multi-source Dijkstra
