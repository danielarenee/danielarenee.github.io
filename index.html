Mathematical epidemiology · KAUST

Preprint

Infection-driven viral mutation and immune escape

Visiting Student Research Program · Numerical Mathematics Group · May–Aug 2026

Developed a compartmental SIRCm model in which infection-linked feedback affects waning immunity and immune escape. The coauthored study compares prevalence-driven and transmission-driven variants and analyzes when endemic equilibria become unstable, even without seasonal forcing.

Operations research · UDLAP

Honors thesis · ongoing

Optimal control of environmental pollution via POMDP

Supervised by Prof. Rubén Blancas Rivera · Feb 2026–present

Developing a partially observable control model for environmental pollution systems. Dynamic programming is used to simulate belief-state policies when measurements are noisy and the system state is not directly observable.

Network science · UACM

Presented · Oct 2026

Inter-city road connectivity across Mexico

Supervised by Prof. Héctor Maravillo · Aug 2024–present

Designing an algorithm to build inter-city connectivity networks from detailed road graphs. The approach combines network contraction with multi-source Dijkstra and is applied to Mexico’s national road network.
